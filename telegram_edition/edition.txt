IGNOU — OAMS Assignment Last Date Extended (TEE December 2026)

IGNOU ke December 2026 Term-End Examination (TEE) ke liye Online Assignment Management System (OAMS) se assignment jama karne ki aakhri taarikh badh gayi hai.

📅 Nayi aakhri taarikh: 24 November 2026
(Pehle aakhri taarikh 20 November 2026 thi.)

Kaun jama kar sakta hai: December 2026 TEE me baithne wale IGNOU ke ODL aur Online programme ke students, jinhone abhi tak assignment jama nahi kiya hai. Jin students ne assignment pehle hi study centre / regional centre par hard copy me jama kar diya hai ya kisi course ka assignment pehle se pass hai, unhe dobara upload karne ki zaroorat nahi hai.

Kaise jama karein: IGNOU Samarth portal (ignou.samarth.edu.in) par login karke OAMS se har course ka assignment ek single PDF (max 20 MB) me upload karein. Email se assignment bhejna sweekar nahi kiya jayega.

📄 Official notice: https://www.ignou.ac.in/viewFile/SED/notification/OAMS_Notification.pdf
(IGNOU Student Evaluation Division, F.No. IG/SED/2026/11243, dated 20 November 2026)

December 2026 TEE 25 November 2026 se shuru ho rahi hai.
